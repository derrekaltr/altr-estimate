Is the business running today?

Operating, winding-down and closed businesses all qualify — just with different buyers. Closed companies are often the fastest deals, because the archive is complete and nothing is still changing.

How big, and how far back do the records go?

These two numbers matter most. Buyers set minimums on record depth — 3+ years is the key threshold — and a larger peak team usually means richer records and bigger offers. Use your peak headcount, even if you're smaller now.

Both numbers are needed — your best estimate is fine.

Where is the business based?

Most buyers currently prioritize US, UK and Canadian businesses with English-language records. Based elsewhere? You can still qualify — the ranges just run lower for now, and we'll tell you honestly.

Which systems hold 3+ years of history?

This is the heart of it. A single system is worth something — but linked history across several (email + tickets + CRM + documents) is what buyers pay a premium for, because it shows how real work actually got done. Check everything that applies; more is better.

What kind of work is in those records?

Specialist and regulated fields — clinical, legal, finance, niche logistics — command a multiple of generic data. Complete workflow chains (a ticket followed from open to resolution, a project start to finish) are especially valuable. Ongoing sales/transaction data opens a separate, recurring route.

Last one — a quick rights check.

Being straight here protects you. None of these automatically disqualify you — they shape which buyers we'd approach and keep you clear of legal trouble. Everything is licensed de-identified either way; EU/UK data just needs a specialist review before anyone can quote it.

Questions people ask

Is this legit?

AI companies pay to license historical business records to train models — micro1, OpenTrain, Nyne and others publish their programs. We run a competitive licensing process: your profile goes in front of several buyers at once, so you get competing written indications instead of one take-it-or-leave-it number.

What does it cost me?

Nothing, ever. The buyer side pays us when a deal closes — if no deal closes, nobody pays anything.

Who sees my information?

Until you send your code, nobody — this page stores nothing and sends nothing. After you send it, one operator reviews it. Your contact details are only shared with a buyer after you've confirmed on a call that you want quotes.

What happens after I send my code?

A human calls or emails you, usually within 2 business days, walks through the details, and — only if you want to proceed — submits your profile to 2–4 buyers in parallel. You compare their written indications and sign only what you like.

Do I have to sell everything? Is it exclusive?

No. You choose what's in scope, everything is licensed de-identified, and we advise never signing anyone's exclusivity letter until all indications are in — it's your main leverage.

How is the person who shared this link involved?

Creators who share this page are paid partners: if a deal closes after they introduce you, they receive a share of what the buyer side pays us. It costs you nothing and doesn't change your numbers.
